# Limitations

Related: [Privacy and security](PRIVACY_AND_SECURITY.md).

- Prospects are potential customers inferred from public signals. They are not confirmed buyers and have not consented to anything ([SKILL.md](../first-customer-finder/SKILL.md), step 4).
- Results depend on live web search and on what is public at that moment; two runs can differ. Evidence can be stale, so the skill is asked to show dates.
- Outreach is drafted, never sent. Sending is manual.
- No automated tests exist. The report generator was only checked with a manual smoke test ([Usage](USAGE.md#verify-the-report-generator)).
- The skill has no version number of its own.
- Private groups, gated sources, data brokers and personal contact discovery are out of scope on purpose.
- Coverage of a given language or market is not guaranteed; the research follows your product input.
